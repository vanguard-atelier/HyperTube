# HyperTube

A distraction-free, privacy-respecting YouTube client tailored for Android tablets and HyperOS.

Built by **Shumail** ([vanguard-atelier](https://github.com/vanguard-atelier)).

---

## What is HyperTube?

Modern streaming apps are intentionally bloated with algorithm-driven feeds, manipulative engagement hooks, shorts, and cluttered navigation. **HyperTube** strips all of that away in favor of a sleek, minimalist media player designed from the ground up for tablets (like the Redmi Pad Pro) and high-resolution displays.

- **Zero Algorithmic Clutter:** No endless homepage dopamine traps. You open the app to watch what you choose or search for.
- **HyperOS Aesthetic:** Deep dark slate themes, HyperOS Blue accents, refined typography, and tablet-optimized viewing layout.
- **Privacy First:** Native playback engine via private Piped infrastructure — no Google account required, no tracking, and no telemetry.
- **Minimalist & Lightweight:** All store bloatware, PR assets, promotional donation overlays, and baseline clutter have been stripped clean.

---

## Architecture & Origin

HyperTube is developed under the Vanguard Atelier ecosystem. It leverages a clean, privacy-oriented native playback pipeline (inspired by the open-source LibreTube player engine) heavily refactored for modern tablet UI/UX, unified dark mode, and an uninterrupted viewing experience.
